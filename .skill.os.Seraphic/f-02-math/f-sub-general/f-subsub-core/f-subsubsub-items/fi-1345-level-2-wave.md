# 🌊 LEVEL 2: WAVE PHYSICS & WAVEGUIDES

## 🌀 THE VIBRATING STRING

Wave physics moves beyond point-masses into the **Continuous Plane**. We simulate the propagation of energy through space and time.

### I. Digital Waveguides (DWG)
- **Problem:** Simulating a string with 0 latency.
- **Solution:** Bi-directional delay lines with scattering junctions.
- **Goal:** Phase-accurate reflection at boundaries.

### II. Karplus-Strong Modeling
- **Mandate:** Excite the waveguide with **Seraphic Noise** (Strophe 14).
- **A0 Rule:** Pre-allocate all delay buffers.

### III. Dispersion & Absorption
- **Law:** Higher frequencies decay faster than lower frequencies.
- **Implementation:** Low-pass filter in the feedback loop with PHI-resonant cutoff.
