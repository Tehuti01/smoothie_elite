# 🧪 LEVEL 1: CLASSICAL MECHANICS & KINEMATICS

## 🌀 THE PHYSICAL ROOT

In the Seraphic ecosystem, "Basic" physics is the bedrock of acoustic modeling. We treat every node as a physical mass with inertia and damping.

### I. Mass-Spring-Damper (MSD) Systems
- **Equation:** `F = ma = -kx - cv`.
- **Implementation:** Use second-order numerical integration (Euler or Runge-Kutta).
- **Goal:** Emulate the movement of speaker cones, microphone diaphragms, and drum skins.

### II. Damping Laws
- **Resonance:** Use `__PHI` to define the decay rate of oscillatory systems.
- **Rule:** Damping coefficients must ensure Ouroboros-stable equilibrium.

### III. Simple Pendulums
- **Use Case:** LFOs with organic timing.
- **Law:** Period is independent of amplitude for small angles (Linear Approximation).
