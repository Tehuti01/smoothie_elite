# 💨 LEVEL 3: FLUID DYNAMICS & ACOUSTIC PRESSURE

## 🌀 THE AIR MANIFOLD

Expert physics treats the air as a fluid. We simulate the non-linear compression and velocity of particles within tubes and resonant cavities.

### I. Navier-Stokes for Audio
- **Problem:** Simulating the "Chuffing" of a bass port or the "Overdrive" of a brass instrument.
- **Solution:** Simplified fluid dynamic solvers (Lattice Boltzmann or FDTD).
- **Goal:** Turbulent noise and non-linear pressure reflection.

### II. Non-Linear Tube Modeling
- **Usage:** Woodwind and Brass instrument synthesis.
- **Path:** Map the cross-sectional area of the instrument to a cascade of scattering junctions.

### III. Room Acoustics (Raytracing)
- **Usage:** High-end Reverbs.
- **Implementation:** SIMD-accelerated raycasting to calculate specular and diffuse reflections.
